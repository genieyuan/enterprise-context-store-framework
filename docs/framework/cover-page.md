# Enterprise Context Store (ECS) — Framework Cover Page

> **ECS is a vendor-neutral framework for turning fragmented enterprise signals and accumulated operating experience into governed, task-ready context for AI agents.**

ECS is designed for AI consumption, not human-facing search. Humans contribute signals,
judgment, corrections, evaluation, and governance; ECS serves compiled context to AI agents.

## The framework

ECS defines four lifecycle stages:

1. **Capture** — acquire selected internal and external enterprise signals completely and traceably.
2. **Compile** — transform signals into linked, evolving enterprise knowledge while preserving evidence, contradictions, uncertainty, and history.
3. **Serve** — supply each AI agent with authorized, task-specific context, including what is known, assumed, disputed, or missing.
4. **Continuous Learning** — use corrections, assumptions, decisions, and outcomes to improve future context under controlled promotion.

**Governance & Trust** is cross-cutting: provenance, permissions, privacy, retention, authority,
temporal validity, auditability, and action boundaries apply throughout.

In short, ECS captures enterprise signals, compiles them into governed context, serves the right
context to AI for each task, and continuously learns from corrections and outcomes. The Context
Store is the product/output consumed at the Serve boundary, not an additional lifecycle stage.

## Boundaries

ECS is not a database, data lake, warehouse, vector store, knowledge graph, enterprise search
service, systems-of-record replacement, agent runtime, or autonomous authority for business
actions. Systems of record continue to own operational facts; ECS primarily supplies the why,
how, history, relationships, exceptions, and learning that help agents reason about those facts.
Context can inform an analysis or proposal, but does not authorize the resulting action.

## Adoption and maturity

Adopt ECS as a framework: select a cross-system AI workflow, identify its signals and outcomes,
map it through **Capture → Compile → Serve → Continuous Learning**, define its Governance & Trust
boundaries, and measure assumptions, repeated questions, corrections, decision time, and outcome
quality before expanding.

This is a technology-agnostic, pre-product framework. Capture and Compile designs are detailed;
Serve, Continuous Learning, Governance & Trust, and end-to-end lifecycle reconciliation remain
open areas of framework development.
